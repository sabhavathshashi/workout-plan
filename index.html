🔁 How to use this plan Do Day 1 → Day 2 → Day 3 → Day 4 → repeat. Since the cycle is continuous, keep Day 3 moderate/skill-focused instead of making every workout maximal.
📌 Basic Rules
⏱️ Warm-up: 5–8 minutes before every workout.
⏱️ Rest: 60–120 sec between normal sets; 2–3 min for hard exercises.
🎯 Most sets: finish with about 1–2 reps left in the tank.
📈 When the top of a rep range becomes easy, progress to a harder variation.
💪 Typical session: 45–70 minutes.
🦴 If joints/tendons feel beat up, reduce volume or intensity rather than forcing it.

📈 Progression System

Don't just repeat the same reps forever. Add reps, sets, or difficulty over time.

PUSH: Regular → Decline → Diamond → Archer → Pseudo-planche → Planche
PULL: Dead hang → Scapular pull-up → Pull-up → Chin-up → Archer pull-up → Muscle-up
SHOULDERS: Pike → Elevated pike → Wall handstand → HSPU progression → Freestanding HSPU
LEGS: Squat → Split squat → Bulgarian → Shrimp squat → Pistol squat
CORE: Hollow hold → Hanging knee raise → Hanging leg raise → Toes-to-bar
🎯 Long-Term Calisthenics Goals
💪 Push: Build toward advanced push-up and planche progressions.
🦍 Pull: Build strong, clean pull-ups and eventually work toward muscle-ups.
⚡ Shoulders: Progress from pike push-ups to handstand push-up variations.
🦵 Legs: Progress toward shrimp squats and pistol squats.
🧱 Core: Progress toward hanging leg raises and toes-to-bar.
